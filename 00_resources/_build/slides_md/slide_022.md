### European Union — Development

- Economic and political partnership
- **28 member states** (at time of slide creation — now 27 post-Brexit)
- Created post-WWII — predominantly economic cooperation + increase trade & prosperity
- **Rule of Law** — everything the EU does is founded on its Treaties
- Concept of the **Single Market** — free movement of people, goods, services and money
- **The Euro** — launched 1.1.99, introduced 1.1.02
- **Food Law** — free movement of foodstuffs
