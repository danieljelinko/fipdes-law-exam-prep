### Voting for EU Law

The EU has **3 legislative (law-making) institutions**:

1. The **European Commission** proposes new laws.
2. These proposals must be passed by both the **European Parliament** and …
3. … the **Council of the European Union**.

#### Qualified Majority Voting (QMV)

In order for a decision to be passed by the Council:

- **At least 55 %** of member states must agree, **and**
- the member states in agreement must represent at least **65 %** of the EU population.
- To **block** a proposal, at least **4 member states** representing at least **35 %** of the EU population must vote against it.
