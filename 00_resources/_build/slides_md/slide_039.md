### Summaries of EU Legislation

*"Summaries of EU legislation"* are short, easy-to-understand explanations of the main legal acts passed by the EU. They are intended for a general, non-specialist audience.

- Most cover the main types of legislation: **directives, regulations and decisions**.
- Some cover other documents, such as international agreements.
- The summaries are grouped into **32 policy fields**, and each links to the full, official version of the act.

*Link:* <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/browse/summaries.html>
