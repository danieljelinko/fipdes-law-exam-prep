### EU Legislation — Where is it published?

- **Google Search** — search in your browser as a quick entry point.
- **European Legislation — EurLex** — the EU reference centre providing online access to European Union Official Journals, treaties, legislation, case law and other documents in **24 languages**.
- **Irish Legislation — Irish Statute Book** — produced by the Office of the Attorney General.
- **Food Legislation** — also available on the **FSAI website**.
- Start with **"Summaries of EU Legislation"** — short, easy-to-understand explanations of the main legal acts, intended for a general, non-specialist audience.
