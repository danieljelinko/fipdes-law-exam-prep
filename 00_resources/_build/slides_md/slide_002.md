### Aim of Module

- Appreciation of the food regulatory environment from the perspective of food production / processing / service.
- Overview of Irish, European and International Food law — identify key pieces of EU legislation.
- Appreciation of legal obligations of food businesses operating in the EU, with particular emphasis on food safety and the food information provided to consumers (labelling, marketing etc.).
- Appreciate the historical evolution of food law.
- Appreciate the origin and context of emerging issues on the food safety agenda.
- Demonstrate awareness of key stakeholders.
