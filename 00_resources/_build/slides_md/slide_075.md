### Risk Communication

**'Risk communication'** means the **interactive exchange of information and opinions** throughout the risk analysis process as regards:

- hazards and risks
- risk-related factors
- risk perceptions

among:

- risk assessors
- risk managers
- consumers
- feed and food businesses
- the academic community
- other interested parties

This includes the explanation of risk assessment findings and the basis of risk management decisions.
