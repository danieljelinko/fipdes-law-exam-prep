### CP versus CCP

What is the difference between a Control Point and a Critical Control Point?

1. A Control Point “CP” is any step in the flow of food production where a physical, chemical or

biological hazard can be controlled.

2. A Critical Control Point or "CCP" is the last step where you can intervene to prevent,

eliminate or reduce a hazard to an acceptable limit.
