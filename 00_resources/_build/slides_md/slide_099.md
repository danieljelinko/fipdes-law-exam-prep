### Who must be identified in the Traceability System? *(cont.)*

> **Process or internal traceability is not required by law.** However, in many businesses it is **best practice** to incorporate process traceability into the scope of the traceability system. Otherwise food business operators risk more extensive **withdrawals / recalls** of unsafe foods.

*Reference: FSAI Guidance Note No. 10.*
