### Link to FSAI Online E-Modules

**Food Safety Authority of Ireland's Learning Portal** — provides training courses, resources, and materials that can be used in conjunction with this module. Create an account to access a range of courses. Once logged in, click on the tab entitled **"Legal Requirements for Food Businesses Courses"**, where you can find the most relevant e-modules:

1. Food Information for Prepacked Foods
2. Nutrition Information under FIC (Reg 1169/2011)
3. Food Additives — The Basics
4. Food Additives — Labelling
5. Food Flavourings — Interviews with the Experts
6. Introduction to Food Contact Materials
7. Regulation 2073/2005 Module 1: Identifying Relevant Microbiological Criteria
8. Regulation 2073/2005 Module 2: Sampling and Testing
