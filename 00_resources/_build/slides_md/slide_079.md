### Article 22 of Regulation (EC) No. 178/2002

- **Article 22 legally established EFSA.**
- Most of EFSA's work is undertaken in **response to requests** for scientific advice from the European Commission, the European Parliament and EU Member States.
- Also carries out scientific work on its **own initiative** — in particular to examine emerging issues and new hazards, and to update its assessment methods and approaches *(known as **"self-tasking"**)*.
