### Safe Food — Discussion

> Do you think that the fact that **"safe food"** is the primary focus of EU and Irish legislation could be problematic?

- What about **nutritionally deficient** food?
- How many people die from a **food-borne illness** compared to **non-communicable disease**?
- Focus is on **dangerous food and harmful food**?
- What about **sustainable** food?
- Focus on **free movement of goods** — individual states cannot intervene?
- How are foods **marketed**?
- Definition of unsafe food has the potential to offer a framework for raising **nutritional qualities** of a food — it is not used in this way *(MacMaolain, 2019)*.
