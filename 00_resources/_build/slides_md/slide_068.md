### Unsafe & Unfit Food — Article 14.5

**Art 14.5** — Consideration should be given to whether the food is **unacceptable for human consumption according to its intended use** for reasons of:

- **Contamination** — whether by extraneous (external) matter or otherwise
- **Putrefaction** (decomposition)
- **Deterioration**
- **Decay** (bacteria / fungi)
