### Example of an In-Store Notification or Paid Newspaper Advertisement

*Source: FSAI Guidance Note 10, Appendix 4, Figure 4.*

The template (headlined **WARNING — Food Recall**) contains:

- **Company name**
- **Food name / description**
- **Pack size**
- **Batch identification**
- **Details of what is wrong with the product**
- **Actions the consumer should take**
- *"We apologise for any inconvenience"*
- **Company address and contact details**
- *Product photograph or illustration*
