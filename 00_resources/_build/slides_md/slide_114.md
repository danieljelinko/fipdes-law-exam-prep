### Example of a Trade Notification Form

*Source: FSAI Guidance Note 10, Appendix 4, Figure 3.*

The template form (titled **URGENT — Food Recall (or Withdrawal)**) captures the following fields:

- **Company Name**
- **Food Name**
- **Food Details**
- **Batch Identification**
- **'Use-by'** or **'Best-before'** date
- **Reasons for the Recall** (free-text)
- **Action Required** (free-text)
- **Contact Details**
- **Alternative Contact Details**
