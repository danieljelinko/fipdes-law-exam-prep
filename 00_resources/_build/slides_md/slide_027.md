### What is EU Law?

EU law is divided into **primary** and **secondary** legislation:

1. **Primary Legislation** — The **treaties** are the basis or ground rules for all EU action.
2. **Secondary Legislation** — Derived from the principles and objectives set out in the treaties; includes:
    1. Regulations
    2. Directives
    3. Decisions
    4. Recommendations
    5. Opinions

Member States have primary responsibility for the correct and timely application of EU Treaties and legislation. The Commission monitors the application of Union law and may take action if a Member State fails to introduce directives or is suspected of breaches.
