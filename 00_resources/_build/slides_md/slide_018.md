### General Principles governing Global Food Safety Law

*Source: Bevilacqua (2015)*

- **Risk Analysis** — *"… a systematic way of gathering, evaluating and recording information which would lead to recommendations, positions or actions in response to an identified hazard."* (WTO, 2000). Four components: risk policy, risk assessment, risk management, risk communication. See SPS Agreement and CAC Procedural Manual.

- **Precautionary Principle** — Enables decision-makers to adopt precautionary measures when scientific evidence about an environmental or human-health hazard is uncertain and the stakes are high. Not formally defined as such, but its requirements are laid out in **Art 7 of Reg (EC) 178/2002**.

- **Principle of Proportionality** — The rule of law is fluid; decision-making should be coherent with the purposes, adequate to reach its tasks, rationally motivated, and directed to cause the least harm to conflicting interests.

- **Principle of Reasonableness** — Adequacy between the legislative prescriptions and the administrative decision.
