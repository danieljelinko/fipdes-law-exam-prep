### How do we manage hazards?

A cascading approach:

1. **Good Hygiene Practices (GHP)** — foundation level.
2. **Procedures based on HACCP** — built on top of GHP.
3. **Regulation 852/2004** — applies to **all food businesses**.
4. **Regulation 853/2004** — applies specifically to **foods of animal origin**.
