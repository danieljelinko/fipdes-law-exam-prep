### EFSA Journal

EFSA gives independent scientific advice on **risk assessment** in the area of food and feed safety, nutrition, animal health and welfare, farm protection and plant health.

**All of its scientific outputs are published in its scientific journal** — the *EFSA Journal* — a **free, online, open-access** publication available on its website, published **monthly**.
