### Food Information Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 — Repeals & Amends

**Repeals:**

1. Directive 2000/13/EC — General Labelling
2. Directive 90/496/EEC — Nutrition Labelling
3. Directive 87/250/EEC — Indication of Alcoholic Strength
4. Directive 1999/10/EC — Certain Derogations for QUID
5. Directive 2002/67/EC — Quinine and Caffeine Declarations
6. Directive 2008/5/EC — Additional Compulsory Indications
7. Regulation (EC) No 608/2004 — added phytosterols, phytosterol esters, phytostanols and/or phytostanol esters

**Amends:**

1. Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006 — Nutrition / Health Claims
2. Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006 — Food Fortification
