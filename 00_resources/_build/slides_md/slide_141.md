### Who do the rules apply to?

1. Applies to **FBO at all stages of the food chain**, where their activities concern the provision of food information to consumers.
2. Includes foods delivered by **mass caterers**, and foods intended for supply to mass caterers.
3. Applies to **catering services provided by transport undertakings** (e.g. airplanes, trains etc.) when the departure takes place on the territories of the Member States to which the Treaties apply.
