### Organic Certification Bodies

- The Organic Certification Bodies (OCBs) provide an inspection and

certification service for all Organic Production Units in Ireland. They
have been designated and are regulated by the Organic Unit of the
Department of Agriculture, Food and the Marine (DAFM), and are
responsible for upholding the Organic standards as defined by the EU.
