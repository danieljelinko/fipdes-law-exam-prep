### Article 3: Definitions

Reg 178/2002 Article 3 defines **18 key terms** used throughout EU food law:

1. Food Law
2. Food Business
3. Food Business Operator
4. Feed
5. Feed Business
6. Feed Business Operator
7. Retail
8. Placing on the market
9. Risk
10. Risk Analysis
11. Risk Assessment
12. Risk Management
13. Risk Communication
14. Hazard
15. Traceability
16. Stages of Production
17. Primary Production
18. Final Consumer
