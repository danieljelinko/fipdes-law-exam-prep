### EFSA and Risk

- **Risk assessment** *(Role for EFSA)*
  - Providing scientific advice on food-related risks to support decision-making.
- **Risk communication** *(also role for EFSA)*
- **Risk management** — *NOT a role for EFSA*. Responsibility for this is with the **Commission** and **Member States**.
