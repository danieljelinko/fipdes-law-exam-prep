### Food Recall Decision Tree

*Source: FSAI Guidance Note 10, Appendix 1.*

The decision tree walks through the FBO's response when a food is suspected of non-compliance:

1. **FBO considers / has reason to believe** that a food it has imported, produced, processed, manufactured or distributed is **not in compliance** with food safety requirements.
2. **Suspend distribution** of the affected food.
3. **Assemble food incident team.**
4. **Collate and analyse all available product, production and quality assurance information** about the affected food.
5. **Determine if the food is unsafe** (see Appendix 2, includes a risk assessment per Appendix 3).
6. Outcome paths:
    - **Food is UNSAFE** → notify competent authorities → **Recall** (if has reached final consumer) or **Withdrawal** (if can be guaranteed not to have reached the consumer).
    - **Food is NOT unsafe but is NOT in compliance with food law** → consult with the competent authorities regarding appropriate control actions.
    - **Food is NOT unsafe and IS in compliance with food law** → monitor situation and handle as a complaint.

*Recall actions:* notify trade customers; notify consumers; remove food from the distribution chain; if necessary to protect public health, remove food from consumers.
*Withdrawal actions:* notify trade customers; remove food from the distribution chain.
