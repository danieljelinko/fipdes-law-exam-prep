### Who must be identified in the Traceability System?

**Supplier Traceability — One Step Back — LEGAL REQUIREMENT**

> Food business operators should be able to identify any "person" from whom they have been supplied with a food, or any product expected to be incorporated into a food. This person may be an individual (e.g. fisherman) or a food business (e.g. an ingredient company).

**Process traceability** — *traceability of food and packaging through the operations within the food business operator's establishment*, whether or not new products are produced. *(Note: this is not a legal requirement but is best practice.)*

**Customer Traceability — One Step Forward — LEGAL REQUIREMENT**

> Food business operators must be able to identify the immediate customers of their products. *(For caterers, this is only applicable when the immediate customer is another business — not when the immediate customer is the final consumer.)*
