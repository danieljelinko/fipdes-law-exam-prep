### EU Law and Irish Law

> European Union legislation is **superior** to all national legislation — European law **takes precedence** over national law.

National infrastructure to cope with the influx of EU Directives, Regulations and Decisions:

- **New Statutes** — laws enacted by the House of Oireachtas pursuant to **Constitution Article 15.2.1**.
- **Statutory Instruments** — a subordinate form of law that can be brought into power by Government (also pursuant to **Constitution Article 15.2.2**).
