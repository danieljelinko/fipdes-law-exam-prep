### General Requirements for a Label

1. **Inform** the purchaser about the product.
2. The purchaser must **not be misled**.
3. Information on the label must be:
    i. **Accurate, clear and easy** to understand.
    ii. Easily **visible**, clearly **legible** & **indelible** (where appropriate).
    iii. **Not hidden**, obscured, detracted from, or interrupted by any other written or pictorial matter or any other intervening material.
