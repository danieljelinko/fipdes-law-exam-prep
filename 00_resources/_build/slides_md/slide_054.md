### Strategic Priorities of the White Paper

1. **Establishment of a new legal framework on food safety legislation** — simplify existing law.
2. **Establishment of an independent European Food Authority (EFSA).**
3. **Establishment of a framework** for the development and operation of national control systems.
4. **Better consumer information.**
5. **International dimension** — Globalisation & international standards; the EU needed a stronger voice.
