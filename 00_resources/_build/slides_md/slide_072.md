### Article 6: Risk Analysis

> In order to achieve the general objective of a **high level of protection of human health and life**, food law shall be based on **risk analysis**.

**Risk Analysis** *(defined in Article 3)* — a process consisting of three interconnected components:

- **Risk assessment**
- **Risk management**
- **Risk communication**
