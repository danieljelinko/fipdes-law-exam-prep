### How are decisions made in the EU?

The EU's standard decision-making procedure is known as the **Ordinary Legislative Procedure** (formerly *"codecision"*):

1. The **European Commission** drafts and implements EU legislation.
2. Both the **European Parliament** (directly elected) and the **EU Council** (28 governments) must approve EU legislation.
