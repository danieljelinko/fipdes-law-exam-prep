### Summary of the Ordinary Legislative Procedure

The procedure flows through these stages:

1. **Commission** drafts a **Proposal**
2. **EU Parliament and Council** receive the proposal
3. **1st Reading**
4. **2nd Reading** — informal negotiation; accept & publish if agreed
5. **Consolidation** — no agreement = rejection; accept & publish if agreed
