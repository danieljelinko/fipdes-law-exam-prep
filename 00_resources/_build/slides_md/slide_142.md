### What is *pre-packed Food*?

**Article 2.1(e):**

- **'Prepacked food'** shall mean any single item for presentation as such to the **final consumer and to mass caterers**, consisting of a food and the packaging into which it was put before being offered for sale, whether such packaging encloses the food completely or only partially, but in any event in such a way that the **contents cannot be altered without opening or changing the packaging**.
- **'Prepacked food' does NOT cover** foods packed on the sales premises **at the consumer's request** or prepacked for **direct sale**.
