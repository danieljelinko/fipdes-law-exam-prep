### Date of Minimum Durability

**’Best before’ date** — Non-perishable foods, long shelf-life. E.g. dry pasta, biscuits, frozen foods.

**’Use by’ date** — From a microbiological point of view, foods that are highly perishable and likely after a short period to constitute an immediate danger to human health. E.g. foods with a short shelf-life that require refrigeration such as milk, minced meat, fish, and ready-to-eat salads.

**Derogations:** e.g. chewing gum, vinegar, bakery products normally consumed within 24 hours.
