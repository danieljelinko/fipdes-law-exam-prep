### Directorate-General for Health & Food Safety (DG SANTE)

- The European Commission is made up of **Directorate-Generals (DGs)**.
- **DG SANTE** (formerly *DG SANCO* — *Santé et Consommateurs*) deals with health and food safety.
- Founded in **October 1999**.
- Primary function: draft and propose EU laws on food and product safety, consumer rights, and health protection — and oversee their application.
