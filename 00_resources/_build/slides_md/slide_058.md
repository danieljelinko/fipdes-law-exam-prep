### General Principles of Food Law — Regulation (EC) No. 178/2002

Three core purposes:

1. Establishes the **common basis** for food law in Member States — common definitions, general provisions and specific requirements.
2. Established the **European Food Safety Authority (EFSA)** and laid down the procedures in matters of food safety.
3. The objective of the Regulation is to ensure a **high level of protection for consumers**, whilst also taking into account:
    - the protection of **animal health and welfare**
    - **plant health**, and
    - **the environment**.
