### Categories of health Claims

Health claims are divided into the following categories:

1. General Health Claims (Article 13) relate to the effect of a substance on a body

function.

2. Article 13(5) claims:

Specific health claims based on newly developed scientific evidence

3. Article 14 claims:

    - a) Claims referring to reduction of disease risk
    - b) Claims referring to children’s development and health e.g. calcium is good for children’s growth
