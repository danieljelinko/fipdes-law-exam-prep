### Risk Communication in Ireland — FSAI (cont.)

**Qualitative Risk Assessment** is delivered through:

- Expert Opinion
- Ad Hoc working groups
- Expert Committees

The assessments are:

- **DoH initiated / FSAI managed**
- **Policy support documents**
- Aligned with **EFSA**

*Example shown on slide:* FSAI *"Scientific Recommendations for Healthy Eating Guidelines in Ireland"* (2011).
