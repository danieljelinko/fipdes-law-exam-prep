### Ongoing Consultation on Risk Assessment

In **January 2018**, the European Commission launched a public consultation seeking the views of stakeholders and citizens on EU risk assessment in the food sector.

An eight-week public consultation was organised via an online questionnaire aiming to gather feedback on the following themes:

1. **Transparency and independence** of the EU risk assessment system with respect to the underlying industry studies and information on which EFSA's risk assessment / scientific advice is based.
2. **Risk communication.**
3. **Governance of EFSA** — in particular the involvement of the EU Member States (MS) in the EU risk assessment system.
