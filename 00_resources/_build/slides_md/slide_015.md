### International Obligations

- **United Nations (UN)** — 1945 — <http://www.un.org/en/index.html>
- **Food & Agricultural Organisation of the United Nations (FAO)** — 1945 — <http://www.fao.org/home/en/>
- **World Health Organisation (WHO)** — 1948 — <https://www.who.int/about/what-we-do>
  *Role: direct and coordinate international health within the United Nations system.*
- **Codex Alimentarius Commission** — 1963 — <http://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/en/>
  *FAO and WHO combined to run this commission, which has established a food code and international standards that ensure food is safe and can be traded.*
- **World Trade Organisation (WTO)** — 1995 — <https://www.wto.org>
  *Established with the main aim of negotiating agreements to reduce obstacles to international trade.*
