### EU Legislation — Three Institutions Involved

- **Council of the EU** — The EU's broad priorities are set by the European Council, which brings together national and EU-level leaders. Governments defend their own country's national interests in the Council of the European Union. Presidency is shared on a rotating basis.

- **European Parliament** — Directly elected MEPs represent European citizens.

- **European Commission** — The interests of the EU as a whole are promoted by the European Commission, whose members are appointed by national governments.
