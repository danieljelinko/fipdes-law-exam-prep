### The BSE Crisis

- First confirmed in cattle in the **UK in 1986** and in **Ireland in 1989**, when 15 cases were confirmed.
- Ireland began culling herds — **152 herds, over 22,400 animals killed** at a cost to the State of **€23.8 m by 1996**.
- In **1996** a link was suggested between BSE and **variant CJD (vCJD)**, impacting humans. CJD is a rare brain disorder leading to dementia, belonging to a group of human and animal **prion disorders**. vCJD can occur after eating meat from diseased cattle.
- Led to UK & Irish government inquiries. BSE most likely spread by cattle eating feed that contained contaminated **Meat and Bone Meal (MBM)** — banned in the 1990s.
- BSE was also discovered in **Germany and Spain in 2000**.
