### National Legislation

Regulation 178 is transposed into Irish national legislation by **two statutory instruments** *(check for updates)*:

| Statutory Instrument | Scope |
|---|---|
| **S.I. No. 22 of 2020** | Food businesses supervised by the **Department of Agriculture, Food and the Marine (DAFM)**, the **Sea-Fisheries Protection Authority (SFPA)** or **Local Authorities** |
| **S.I. No. 747 of 2007** | Food businesses supervised by the **Health Service Executive (HSE)** |
