### QUID Is Required When:

- The ingredient is **included in the name of the food** e.g. cheese & onion quiche (cheese & onion must be quantified)
- The ingredient is **associated with the name** of the food such as ‘summer pudding’ (the fruit content must be quantified)
- The ingredient is **emphasised** in words, pictures or graphics such as ‘with cheese’ (the cheese content must be quantified)

> **Note:** a serving suggestion does not need a QUID

**Example:** Ingredients: Whole milk, Pasteurised egg, Wheat flour, Cheese **(15%)**, Onion **(12%)**, Vegetable oil, Hydrogenated vegetable oil, Modified maize starch, Salt, Dextrose
