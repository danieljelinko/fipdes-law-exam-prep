### Article 2: Definition of *Food*

> **'Food'** means any substance or product, whether processed, partially processed or unprocessed, **intended to be, or reasonably expected to be ingested by humans**.

**'Food' includes:**

- drink
- chewing gum
- and any substance, including water, intentionally incorporated into the food during its manufacture, preparation or treatment.

**'Food' shall NOT include:**

a. feed
b. live animals (unless they are prepared for placing on the market for human consumption)
c. plants prior to harvesting
d. medicinal products
e. cosmetics
f. tobacco
