a.

All types of authorised health claims  (13.1, 13.5, 14.1a, 14.1b) are all listed in the Community Register of
Claims for the EU. You can search this register here, for claims relating to the nutrients you have identified
for your product.
Each authorised health claim has specific ‘conditions of use’ and these are listed in the Community
Register of Claims. You need to check that your food product satisfies the conditions of use for each claim
in order to use it legally.
