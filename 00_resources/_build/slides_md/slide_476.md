### Sprouts & Seeds

Following the large outbreak in France and Germany of Shiga toxin-producing E. Coli in May
2011, consumption of sprouts was identified as the most likely origin of the outbreaks. This has
resulted in a suite of measures in the form of four new Commission Regulations to cover the
import, traceability, microbiological criteria and approval of establishments producing sprouts.
