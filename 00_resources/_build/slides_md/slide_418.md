### COMMISSION NOTICE on the implementation of FSMS covering GHP and procedures based on the HACCP

principles, including the facilitation/flexibility of the implementation in certain food businesses (2022/C 355/01)

In 2016, the Commission adopted the Commission Notice on the implementation of food safety
management systems covering prerequisite programmes (PRP) and procedures based on the
HACCP principles, including the facilitation/ flexibility of the implementation in certain food
businesses – this notice provided guidance ... Since 2016, a number of revisions of relevant
legislation took place (e.g. the introduction of allergen control and food safety culture as
requirements in Regulation (EC) No 852/2004 (2) by Regulation (EU) 2021/382) and international
standards (e. g. the revision of ISO 22000 and of the Codex Alimentarius General principles of
Food Hygiene, and the adoption of the Codex Alimentarius Code of Practice on Food Allergen
Management for Food Business Operators). A number of relevant scientific opinions were
published by the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) and further experiences were also
gained from the practical implementation of the recommendations.
