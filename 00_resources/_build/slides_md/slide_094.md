### FSAI Guidance Note 10 — Product Recall and Traceability (Revision 3)

- Outlines the **legal requirements** for the traceability of food and recall / withdrawal of unsafe food within the meaning of **Regulation (EC) No. 178/2002**.
- Provides guidance on **best practice** for the development of traceability systems and the management of food recalls or withdrawals, where the legislation has been non-prescriptive.
