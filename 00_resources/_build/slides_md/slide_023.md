### The Need for a European Policy

EU Food law developed **incrementally** over a long period of time.

- The **Treaty of Rome** made no explicit mention of consumer protection or public health.
- Not until the **Single European Act (1986)** and the **Maastricht Treaty (1992)** were consumer protection and public health introduced as EU goals.
- Food law concentrated on the **free movement of foodstuffs** in the earlier years.

Key milestones:

- **1962** — The first EU food Directive, concerned with colours in foodstuffs.
- **1979** — *Cassis de Dijon* and other cases pushed the EU to reorientate its food law.
