### How long do you keep records?

| Statutory Instrument | Type of business | Record retention | Supervised by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S.I. No. 747 of 2007** (as amended) | All food businesses | Maintain records for all food **at least until it can be reasonably assumed that the food has been consumed** — typically **6 months – 5 years** | **HSE** |
| **S.I. No. 432 of 2009** | Generally processors, manufacturers and cold stores handling **foods of animal origin** (including seafood) | **3 years** in all cases | **DAFM** |
