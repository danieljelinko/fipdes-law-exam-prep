### The 27 Countries of the EU — Enlargement Timeline

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1958** | EEC formed with 6 countries: Belgium, France, Germany, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands |
| **1973** | Denmark, Ireland, United Kingdom join |
| **1981** | Greece joins |
| **1986** | Spain, Portugal join |
| **1995** | Austria, Finland, Sweden join |
| **2004** | Czech Republic, Cyprus, Estonia, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Poland, Slovakia, Slovenia join (largest enlargement) |
| **2007** | Bulgaria, Romania join |
| **2013** | Croatia joins |
| **2016** | **BREXIT** referendum |
| **2020** | UK Withdrawal Agreement — UK formally leaves the EU |
