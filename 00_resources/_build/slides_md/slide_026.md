### The EU Institutions

The EU is composed of the following institutions and bodies:

- **European Council** (Summit)
- **European Parliament**
- **Council of the EU**
- **European Commission**
- **Court of Justice**
- **Court of Auditors**
- **Economic and Social Committee**
- **Committee of the Regions**
- **European Investment Bank**
- **European Central Bank**
- **Agencies**
