### The Need for a European Policy (cont.)

- **1985** — Completion of the Internal Market: Community Legislation on Foodstuffs covering Public Health, Consumer Information and Controls of Foodstuffs.
- **BSE Crisis (1986 onwards)** — call for safe food on the EU market.
- **Green Paper 1997** — called for Food Law to address:
    - public health
    - free circulation of goods
    - scientific basis (evidence and risk assessment)
    - competitiveness, HACCP
