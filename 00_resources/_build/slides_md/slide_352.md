### The Hygiene Package cont.

4. Council Directive 2002/99 lays down animal-health rules governing the

production, placing on the market and importation of products of animal origin
intended for human consumption.

5. Directive 2004/41/EC repeals certain directives food hygiene and health

conditions for the production and placing on the market of certain products of
animal origin intended for human consumption and amends Council Directives
89/662/EEC and 92/408/EC, 21 April 2004
