### EU Food Labelling System (FLIS) information-

- The Food Labelling Information System provides a user friendly IT

solution which enables its users to select the food and automatically
retrieve the mandatory EU labelling indications in 23 EU languages.
The system also provides links to the relevant legal provisions and
existing guidance documents:
