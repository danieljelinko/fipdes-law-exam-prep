### Indicative Syllabus

1. Introduction to Food Regulatory Affairs, the EU and its institutions
2. International Perspective and Codex Alimentarius
3. Interaction of International, European and National Law and role of Case Law
4. Background to evolution of EU law (BSE Crisis)
5. The regulatory environment, enforcement and food inspection agencies
6. Food Agencies and the European Food Authority (role of FSAI, EFSA and other agencies)
7. Official Control of Foodstuffs & Veterinary Office Unit
8. HACCP and Food Hygiene
9. Food Information to Consumers and Food Labelling
10. Nutrition Declaration & Health Claims
11. Food Quality / Additives / Foods for Particular Uses (PARNUTS) / Geographical Indication / Organic / GMOs and Genetically Modified Foods
12. Emerging issues — food fraud, RASFF, voluntary labelling and marketing terms
