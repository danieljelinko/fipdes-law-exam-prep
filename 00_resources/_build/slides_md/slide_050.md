### Dioxin Crisis

- **Dioxins and PCBs** (Polychlorinated biphenyls) are toxic chemicals that can provoke serious health effects — cancer, hormone disruption, reduced ability to reproduce, skin toxicity and immune system disorders — if exposure continues over an extended period (years).
- PCBs are formed during **combustion (burning) processes** and as **by-products of industrial processes**.
- The dioxin crisis struck in **Belgium during the spring of 1999**.
- Related to **contamination of feedstock with PCB**, which had been detected in food products — mainly chicken and eggs (contaminants tend to accumulate in fatty tissues).
- Health inspectors reported the case in **January**, but measures were only taken in **May 1999** — a critical delay.
