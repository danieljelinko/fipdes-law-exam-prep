### EU Relations with the United Kingdom — *What is BREXIT?*

- EU page on relations with the UK: <https://ec.europa.eu/info/relations-united-kingdom_en>
- BBC explainer (summary in simple, clear format): <https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-46318565>

The UK left the EU at the end of **31 January 2020 CET**. This began a transition period that ended on **31 December 2020 CET**, during which the UK and EU negotiated their future relationship.

**Further reading:** O'Rourke (2018), *A Short History of Brexit from Brentry to Backstop*, Pelican, Great Britain.
