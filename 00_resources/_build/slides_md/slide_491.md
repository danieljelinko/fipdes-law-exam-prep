### New EU Legislation on Seeds

- To mitigate risk as a result of this case, additional requirements for

sprouting seeds are now required.

- Suite of new EU Regulations:
- 208/2013 on traceability
- 209/2013 on microbiological criteria
- 210/2013 on approval of establishments producing sprouts
- 2020/2235 establishes standard models for entry of sprouts into EU
- 2022/2292 food producing animals
- 2023/2652 honey, meat, refined products, gelatine, fishery
