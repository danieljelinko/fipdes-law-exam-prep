### Application of Rules

1. **Prepacked foods** available for sale to consumers and/or catering trade.
2. **Derogations** for:
    i. Food sold **loose**, or
    ii. Food packaged **on the premises** from where it is sold, or
    iii. Packaged **at the request of the consumer**.
3. **Business-to-Business** sales.
