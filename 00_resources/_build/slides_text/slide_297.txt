Fortification with vitamins and minerals

• Some nutrients or ingredients (Vitamins, Minerals including trace

elements, Amino acids, Essential fatty acids, Fibre, Various plants and
herbal extracts) can be added to food in order to "enrich" or "fortify"
the food in question, so as to add or emphasise particular nutritional
characteristics.

• Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006 harmonises the provisions regarding

the addition of vitamins and minerals and of certain other substances
to foods.

• Regulation 1925/2006 has been aligned with the new Regulatory

Committee procedure with Scrutiny by Regulation (EC) No 108/2008.
