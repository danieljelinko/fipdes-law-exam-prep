The Need for a
European Policy

EU Food law developed incrementally over a long period of time.

Treaty of Rome made no explicit mention of consumer protection or
public health

Not until Single European Act 1986 and Maastricht Treaty of 1992
were consumer protection and public health introduced as goals.

Food Law concentrated on the free movement of foodstuffs in the
earlier years.

1962:  The first EU food Directive – concerned with colours in
foodstuffs.

1979:  Cassis de Dijon and other cases – pushed EU to reorientate its
food law.
