Secondary
Legislation:
Non-Binding

Recommendations:  A "recommendation" is not binding.

Opinions:  An "opinion" is an instrument that allows the institutions to make a
statement in a non-binding fashion, in other words without imposing any legal
obligation on those to whom it is addressed. An opinion is not binding.

But what can non-binding secondary legislation achieve?

∙  Allow EU institutions make their view known and suggest course of action.

∙  Influence policy.

∙  Some recommendations and opinions may later feed into mandatory rules.
