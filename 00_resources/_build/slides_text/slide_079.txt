Article 22 of
Regulation EC
(No) 178/2002

• Article 22 legally established EFSA

• Most of EFSA’s work is undertaken in
response to requests for scientific
advice from the European
Commission, the European
Parliament and EU Member States.

• Also carries out scientific work on
their own initiative, in particular to
examine emerging issues and new
hazards and to update their
assessment methods and approaches
(“self-tasking”).
