How much should be withdrawn or recalled?

Assume that all food in that batch is unsafe unless there is
evidence to the contrary (Article 14.3 Regulation
178/2002)

Batch – means a group or set of identifiable products
obtained from a given process under practically identical
circumstances and produced in a given place within one
defined production period (Regulation (EC) 2073/2005)

Note:  If your batch size is small and narrowly defined, and
you have a more detailed traceability system, you will have
a smaller product withdrawal or recall.
