Q: What stages of the food chain is it applicable to?
                  A: Applies to all stages of production, processing and
                  distribution of food

                  Q: Who does it apply to?
Reg 178/2002      A: “Food business” which is defined as any undertaking,
                  whether for profit or not and whether public or private,
                  carrying out any of the activities related to any stage of
Chp 2: Scope of   production, processing and distribution of food.
Legislation
                  Q: Are there any exemptions?
                  A: Primary production for private domestic use or to the
                  domestic preparation, handling or storage of food for private
                  domestic consumption.
