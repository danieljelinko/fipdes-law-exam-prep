Risk Analysis - “ … a systematic way of gathering, evaluating and recording information which
would lead to recommendations, positions or actions in response to an identified hazard.” WTO
(2000).  Four components:  risk policy, risk assessment, risk management and risk
communication.  See SPS Agreement and CAC Procedural Manual.

Precautionary Principle – The precautionary principle enables decision-makers to adopt
precautionary measures when scientific evidence about an environmental or human health
hazard is uncertain and the stakes are high.  It is not defined as such but its requirements laid
out in Art 7 Reg 178/2002/EC

Principle of Proportionality – rule of law is fluid – decision making should be coherent with the
purposes, adequate to reach its tasks, rationally motivated and directed to cause the least harm
to conflicting interests.
