Reg 178/2002

# Chp 2: Scope of Legislation

Q: What stages of the food chain is it applicable to?

A: Applies to all stages of production, processing and distribution of food

Q: Who does it apply to?

A: "Food business" which is defined as any undertaking, whether for profit or not and whether public or private, carrying out any of the activities related to any stage of production, processing and distribution of food.

Q: Are there any exemptions?

A: Primary production for private domestic use or to the domestic preparation, handling or storage of food for private domestic consumption.
