# Ongoing Consultation on Risk Assessment

In Jan 2018, the European Commission launched a public consultation seeking the views of stakeholders and citizens on EU risk assessment in the food sector.

Eight-week public consultation was organised via an online questionnaire aiming to gather feedback on the following themes:

1. transparency and independence of the EU risk assessment system with respect to the underlying industry studies and information on which EFSA's risk assessment/scientific advice is based.
2. risk communication.
3. the governance of EFSA, in particular the involvement of the EU Member States (MS) in the EU risk assessment system.
