ARTICLE 1: SCOPE

Applies to all stages of production, processing and distribution of food and to exports

This includes...

- The sale of food via the internet
- Certain businesses specialising in trading food (brokers)
- Primary production &amp; associated operations: i.e. the production, rearing or growing of primary products including harvesting, milking and farmed animal production prior to slaughter. It also includes hunting and fishing and the harvesting of wild products (defined in Regulation (EC) 178/2002)
