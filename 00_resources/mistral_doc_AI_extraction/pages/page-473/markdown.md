# Week 9 (Revision Topics)

1. Using a case studies from closure orders, debate where the responsibility for preventing food business closure orders in Ireland should lie, is it primarily with businesses rather than regulators or consumers, particularly in the implementation of effective FSMS (including food safety, traceability, safety culture, and staff training).

2. Research contamination and/or fraud case studies such as the Horse Meat Scandal, Ecoli etc. Debate whether regulators, rather than consumers, should bear the primary responsibility for responding to food fraud and contamination incidents in the EU, including ensuring effective traceability, withdrawal/recall systems, RASFF coordination, and communication.

3. Debate whether flexibility in the design of Food Safety Management Systems benefits food companies more than it risks undermining food safety standards.

4. Cross Contamination: debate whether food manufacturers should be held fully accountable for preventing cross-contamination in the context of allergens, gluten-free in products placed on the market, regardless of operational challenges. Indicate how this impacts labelling of food products.
