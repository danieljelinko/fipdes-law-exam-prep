# Link to FSAI online learning embodies

- Food Safety Authority of Ireland’s Learning Portal: Here you will find training courses, resources, and materials that can be used in conjunction with this module. By creating a new account here, you can access a range of courses. 2. Nutrition Information under FIG (Reg 1169/2011)
3. Food Additives - The Basics
4. Food Additives - Labelling
5. Food Flavourings - Interviews with the Experts
6. Introduction to Food Contact Materials
7. Regulation 2073/2005 Module 1: Identifying Relevant Microbiological Criteria
8. Regulation 2073/2005 Module 2: Sampling and Testing
