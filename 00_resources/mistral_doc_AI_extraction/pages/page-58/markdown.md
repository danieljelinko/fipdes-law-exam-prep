# General Principles of Food Law Regulation (EC) No. 178/2002

Establishes the common basis for food law in Member States and includes common definitions, general provisions and specific requirements.

Established the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) and laid down the procedures in matters of food safety.

The objective of the Regulation is to ensure a high level of protection for consumers whilst also taking into account:

the protection of animal health and welfare,

plant health and

the environment.
