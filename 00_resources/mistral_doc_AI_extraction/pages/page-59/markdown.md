# National Legislation

Regulation 178 is transposed into national legislation by two statutory instruments (check for updates):

S.I. No. 22 of 2020 for food businesses supervised by the Department of Agriculture, Food and Marine (DAFM), the Sea-Fisheries Protection Authority (SPFA) or Local Authorities

S.I. No. 747 of 2007 for food businesses supervised by the Health Services Executive (HSE)
