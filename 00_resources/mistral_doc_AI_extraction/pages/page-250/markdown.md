# Nutritional Labelling

## What must appear in the nutrition declaration?

Information on the following must be declared per 100g/ml of the final product: Energy (kJ/kcal), Fat (g), Saturates, Carbohydrates (g), Sugars (g), Protein (g) and Salt (g)

## How must the information be presented?

Nutrition information must be presented in a tabular format with the numbers aligned. Where the space on the packaging does not allow for this, the declaration may appear in linear format.
