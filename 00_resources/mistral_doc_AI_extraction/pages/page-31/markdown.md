# How are decisions made in the EU

☐ The EU’s standard decision-making procedure is known as the Ordinary Legislative Procedure (ex “codecision”):

1. The European Commission drafts and implements EU legislation.
2. Both the European Parliament (directly elected) and the EU Council (28 governments) has to approve EU legislation.
