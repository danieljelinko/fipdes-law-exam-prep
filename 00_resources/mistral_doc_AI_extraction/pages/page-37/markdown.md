# EU Law and Irish Law

European Union legislation is superior to all national legislation.

This means that European law is superior and takes precedence over national law.

National infrastructure to cope with influx of EU Directives, Regulations and Decisions:

- New Statutes: Laws enacted by House of Oireachtas pursuant to the Constitution Article 15.2.1
- Statutory Instruments: subordinate form of law that can be brought into power by Government (also pursuant to the Constitution Article 15.2.2)
