# Chapter II
Food Business Operators obligations

FBOs obligations...

1. FBOs must comply with the requirements of Annex II (Identification Marking) and III (Specific requirements for slaughtering, cutting, boning etc)
2. Establishments handling products for which Annex III lays down requirements must be **approved** by the competent authority
3. A product of animal origin originating in an establishment subject to approval cannot be placed on the market unless a **health mark or identification mark** is applied
