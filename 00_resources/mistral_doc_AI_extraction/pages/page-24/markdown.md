EU: Free Movement of Goods

Principe of Mutual Recognition (Casis de Dijon Case)

Once a food product is legally marketable in one member State, it must be (subject to certain exceptions), be legally marketable in all EU member States.

Repercussions:

- Harmonisation
- Generic Foods Foodstuffs
- Low common denominator (Quality)
- Locally and artisan production methods come under threat
- Larger manufacturers are in a stronger position to lobby
- Foods from outside EU – WTO and International Concerns
- Food Nutrition – Animal Welfare – Human Rights
