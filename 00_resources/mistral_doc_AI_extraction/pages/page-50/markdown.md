# Dioxin Crisis

- Dioxins and PCBs (Polychlorinated biphenyls) are toxic chemicals that can provoke serious health effects such as cancer, hormone disruption, reduced ability to reproduce, skin toxicity and immune system disorders if exposure continues over an extended period (i.e. a number of years).
- PCBs formed during combustion (burning) processes and as by-products of industrial processes
- Dioxin crisis struck in Belgium during the spring of 1999.
- Related to contamination of feedstock with (PCB) which had been detected in food products, mainly chicken and eggs (contaminants tend to accumulate in fatty tissues)
- Health inspectors reported the case in January, measure only taken in May 1999
