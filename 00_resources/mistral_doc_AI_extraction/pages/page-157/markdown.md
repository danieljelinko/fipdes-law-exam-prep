# E) Specific Provisions – Annex VI

- ☐ Meat products/meat preparations/fishery products with added proteins/different animal protein - name of the food shall bear an indication of the presence of those proteins and of their origin.
- ☐ Meat products/meat preparations having appearance of a cut/joint/slice/portion/carcase – name shall include an indication of presence of added water if it makes up &gt;5 % of weight of the finished product
- ☐ Fishery products/prepared fishery products having appearance of a cut/joint/slice/portion/ filet/whole fishery product – name shall include an indication of presence of added water if it makes up &gt;5 % of weight of the finished product
