# Primary Products

1. Products of plant origin, e.g. grains, fruits, vegetables, herbs, mushrooms.
2. Products of animal origin, e.g. eggs, raw milk, honey, fishery products, live bivalve molluscs.
3. Products harvested wild either from plant or animal origin, e.g. mushrooms, berries, snails etc.

**NOTE:**

☐ Fresh meat is not a primary product since it is obtained after slaughter.

☐ Fishery products remain primary products even after slaughter, bleeding, handling, gutting, removing fins, refrigeration and placing in containers for transport at the level of primary production.

☐ Cheese is the result of processing raw or heat treated milk &amp; is therefore not a primary product.

See EC Guidance document 852/2004
