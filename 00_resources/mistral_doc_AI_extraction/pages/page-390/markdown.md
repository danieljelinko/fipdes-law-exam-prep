# Article 5.2: HACCP Principles

1. Identifying any hazards e.g. survival of E.coli O157 due to inadequate cooking of a minced meat/burger.
2. Identifying the critical control points (CCP) i.e. the cooking step
3. Establishing critical limits i.e. 75°C at the core.
4. Establishing and implementing effective monitoring procedures at critical control points i.e. periodically check that the correct temperature has been reached
5. Establishing corrective actions e.g. Cook for longer, i.e. until correct temperature is reached.
6. Verification Check periodically that staff are monitoring CCPs and taking corrective action.
7. Document Keep monitoring records
