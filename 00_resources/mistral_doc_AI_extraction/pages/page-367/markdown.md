|  RISK CATEGORISATION OF BUSINESS | Reason for category | Examples of businesses  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  HIGH-RISK | Where the potential exists to put vulnerable groups (infants, elderly, pregnant women & the sick) or large numbers of consumers at risk | •Shellfish production;
•Manufacturing / processing of baby food, meat & milk products, cook-chill products, salads;
•Fish products / cooked meat distribution;
•Delicatessen;
•Hospital catering;
•Butcher (mixed products)  |
|  MEDIUM- RISK | Where high-risk ready-to-eat foods are not prepared, but the scale of business is large | •Manufacturing / processing of oils & fats, chocolate products
•Frozen food distributor, cold stores, cash & carry
•Health food
•shop, fish monger (raw only)
•B&B (>6 people)
•Butcher (raw meat only)  |
|  LOW-RISK | Where the potential to cause harm to consumers is low. | •Honey production
•Manufacturing / processing of cereal products,
•jam & jelly
•Dry goods distribution
•Greengrocer
•B&B (<6 people)
•Bakery  |
