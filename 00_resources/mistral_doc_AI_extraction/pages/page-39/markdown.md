# Summaries of EU Legislation

"Summaries of EU legislation" are short, easy-to-understand explanations of the main legal acts passed by the EU. These are intended for a general, non-specialist audience. Most cover the main types of legislation passed by the EU: directives, regulations and decisions. Some cover other documents, such as international agreements. The summaries are grouped into 32 policy fields, and each links to the full, official version of the act. Here is the link:

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/browse/summaries.html
