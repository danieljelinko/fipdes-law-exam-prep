# Nutrients (Macro and Micro)

## Regulation No 1169/2011 defines nutrient as:

- Protein
- Carbohydrate
- Fat
- Fibre
- Sodium
- Vitamins and minerals listed in point 1 of Part A of Annex XIII to this Regulation (see page 9) and substances which belong to or are components of one of those categories
