# CABERNET SAUVIGNON

# HODGSON &amp; SPROCKET

This finely crafted wine comes from grapes grown in our back yard. The unique terroir of this urban landscape combines with the balmy Wisconsin climate to create far from ideal growing conditions. Despite these hardships the hardy cabernet variety seems to thrive and each year produces several grapes and occasionally an entire bunch. These grape are crushed by hand and the resulting ounces of juice added to the finest wine kit available to create this unique 100% cabernet blend. The wines exhibits classic plum and raspberry notes and has a hell of a kick.

Enjoy.

13.5% ALC/VOL 750ML
