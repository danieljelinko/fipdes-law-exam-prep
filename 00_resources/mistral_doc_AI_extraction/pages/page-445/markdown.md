# Scope (Products)

3. **Does not** apply to food containing **both** products of plant origin and processed products of animal origin e.g. dairy chocolate, bakery products, pizzas etc.

- Processing of a composite product is therefore subjected to the hygiene requirements of Regulation (EC) 852/2004 only **however**, the handling of an unprocessed ingredient thereof which is of animal origin must comply with the requirements of Regulation (EC) 853/2004
