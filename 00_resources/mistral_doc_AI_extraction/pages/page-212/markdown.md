# DRVs for Sodium and Chloride 2019

The EFSA Panel concludes that a Sodium intake of 2 grams per day is the adequate intake for the general adult population, while for Chloride the adequate intake for adults is considered to be 3.1 grams per day. The given levels of both nutrients correlate since they are obtained from Salt (Sodium Chloride) as the main dietary source.
