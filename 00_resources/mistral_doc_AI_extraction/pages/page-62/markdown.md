# Reg 178/2002 Chp 2: Scope of Legislation

Food Businesses must:

1. Not place food on the market that is unsafe
a. Injurious to health
b. Unfit for human consumption

2. Not advertise, present or label food in a way that misleads the consumer

3. Ensure that foods satisfy the requirements of applicable food law

4. Record traceability information in a systematic way

5. Initiate withdrawal and recall unsafe products

6. Notify competent authorities of withdrawal/recall

7. Ensure food imported into EU complies with EU food law

8. Ensure food exported from EU complies with EU food law unless 3rd country has own specifications
